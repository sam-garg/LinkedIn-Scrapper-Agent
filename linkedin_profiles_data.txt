🌟 Honoured to Be Part of the 17th International Conference on Intelligent Human-Computer Interaction (IHCI 2025) 🌟

From 14–16 November, I had the incredible opportunity to attend the 17th IHCI Conference held at Hotel Marriott, Jaipur, organised by Banasthali Vidyapith — an experience I will truly remember for a lifetime.

The conference brought together brilliant minds from across the world, and I had the privilege to listen, learn, and interact with some of the most influential researchers and leaders in AI & HCI.

🔹 Day 1 Highlights
One of the most insightful sessions for me was the talk by Prof. Javed I. Khan (Kent State University, USA) on Conversational Ethics and the Sovereign AI Dilemma.
During the Q&A, I asked a question on whether AI can be tricked or manipulated into performing actions it ideally shouldn’t — and receiving appreciation for my question from Prof. Javed himself was a moment of true encouragement for me.

I also had meaningful interactions with:
• Dr. Shivpal Singh (DRDO) — where we discussed applications of AI in modern missile systems.
• Prof. KC Santosh (University of South Dakota, USA) — whose work on sustainable and human-inspired AI deeply resonated with me.
• Prof. Wan-Young Chung (South Korea) and several others whose research broadened my understanding of the global AI landscape.

Across the three days, the conference covered powerful themes like:
✔ Ethical & Responsible AI
✔ Human-Centered & Sustainable AI
✔ AI in Defense and National Security
✔ Advances in Vision, Speech & Interactive Technologies
✔ Human–AI Collaboration
✔ Generative AI, Cybersecurity, NLP for Social Good, and more.

We also got the chance to enjoy authentic Rajasthani cuisine at Chokhi Dhani, and the final day concluded with crisp sessions on Generative AI, cybersecurity in HCI, and emerging directions in Human–AI collaboration.

✨ Gratitude
I am truly grateful to Banasthali Vidyapith for giving me this opportunity, and to Prof. Anshuman Shastri Sir and Ena Ma’am for organising everything so seamlessly and supporting us throughout the journey.
My heartfelt thanks to the LitWits Community for extending this opportunity, and a special acknowledgment to Dr Monika Jain Ma’am, through whose role as the head of the Litwits Community we were able to be a part of this experience.

These three days were filled with learning, curiosity, global perspectives, and conversations that will stay with me forever. Truly honoured to have been part of IHCI 2025. 🌟

"Not every test teaches you a new algorithm. Some teach you how to think."

Hi, Connections! 👋

I wanted to share a recent experience that reminded me there's always something new to learn.

A few days ago, I was excited to be shortlisted for the Amazon ML Summer School 2026 assessment after clearing the SOP screening round. Today, I appeared for the online test.

The assessment was designed to test much more than coding skills:
✨ 2 LeetCode-style coding problems
✨ 20 MCQs covering aptitude, mathematics, and core concepts
⏱️ All under strict time constraints, a minute for the mcqs, and only 20mins for the coding problems, where every minute mattered.

I found myself deeply invested in one of the coding questions, trying different approaches and looking for the optimal solution. Before I knew it, time had slipped away.

Will I make it to the next round? Honestly, I don't know.

But if there's one lesson I'd love to share with anyone preparing for similar assessments, it's this:

--> Knowing the solution is only half the battle. Knowing when to move on is just as important.

Time management, prioritization, and staying calm under pressure are skills we often underestimate, yet they're what many competitive assessments are really evaluating.

I'm grateful to Amazon for this opportunity and for the lessons that came with it. Every challenge—whether it ends in success or not—adds another layer to your growth.

For now, it's back to the grind. More DSA, more problem-solving, more timed practice, and a better version of myself with every question I solve.

The journey continues. 🚀

About

Technology is most useful when it solves real problems. That's what motivates the way I build software and AI applications.
I'm an AI and Software Engineer with experience developing intelligent applications, automation tools, and data-driven solutions using Python, Oracle APEX, PL/SQL, SQL, and Generative AI. My work focuses on combining software engineering with AI to simplify complex workflows, improve productivity, and make information easier to access.
